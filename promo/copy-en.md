## Reddit (r/DeepSeek or similar)

**Title:** I made 20 literary/artistic themes for DeepSeek Harness (open source)

**Body:**
DSH is great but the default look gets old. I built a theme plugin with 20 original themes — ink wash, celadon, parchment, moonlit, watercolour, colour field, oil dusk, silver film, spring river, bamboo wind, morning page, sea horizon, lantern night, starry journey, ancient path, lotus dawn, home smoke, rain tea, summit dawn, paper wish.

Each theme is a full 116-token light/dark palette with a matched animated wallpaper, plus three background-clarity levels, fixed/carousel modes and an upload-your-own-wallpaper flow.

- Live demo (no install): https://ruohan0713.github.io/dsh-theme-studio/

- npm: `npx @deepseek-ai/dsh plugin add dsh-theme-studio-art`
